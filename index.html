GUIDE

Objective

Defend the central base for as long as possible while expanding your economy and defenses. Enemies continuously spawn and become more dangerous over time.

Camera & Pause

Arrow keys pan the battlefield. On mobile, drag the map. Press Space or the large Pause button to pause. You can still issue orders and plan construction while paused.

Movable Units

Click/tap a squad or truck to select it, then click/tap a destination. Units path around terrain and buildings. The ✕ beside a selected unit clears the selection.

Resources

Killing aliens gives Gold. Crystal Mines snap onto crystal deposits; Trucks carry crystal to Landing Pads where ships export it for Gold. Ore Mines feed Refineries, which create Metal.

Gold

Gold buys movable units and economy buildings such as Mines, Refineries and Landing Pads.

Metal

Metal builds combat Towers and Walls, and upgrades existing Towers. Establish Ore Mine → Truck → Refinery routes to sustain defensive expansion.

Tower Upgrades

Tap a built combat Tower to select it. Every Tower starts at 1/3 and can be upgraded twice with Metal. Higher levels add tower-specific mechanics instead of only small stat increases.

Truck Routes

Select a Truck and record at least two route points. When finished, the Truck drives to the first point and automatically begins repeating the route.

Walls

Select Wall, then click bend points or drag to paint a path. Press ✓ to review total metal cost and build time. Press ✕ to cancel the current wall path.

Landing Pads

Ships periodically land at Landing Pads, load deposited crystal and launch when full. A landing ship attracts nearby aliens, so protect the pad.

Flyers

Flyers begin appearing after the run gets underway. They ignore terrain and Walls completely. Anti-Air Cannons are the most metal-efficient answer, although several other weapons can still hit them.

Specialized Towers

Cryo controls crowds, Mortars bombard groups at long range, Miniguns shred single targets, Missiles deliver heavy splash damage and Drone Bays create autonomous local defense. Anti-Air Cannons specialize against Flyers.
